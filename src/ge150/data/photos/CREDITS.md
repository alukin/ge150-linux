# Gear photos

Photos of the original gear each model is based on, all from Wikimedia Commons under a free
licence (listed for each one), converted to WebP. They belong to their authors and are not
covered by the project's MIT licence. Brand and product names belong to their owners.

Only freely licensed photos are shipped: gear without one is drawn by the editor, and users
can add their own photos in `~/.local/share/ge150/photos/` (`<gear id>.jpg`, `.png` or
`.webp`). Rows marked `(= id)` reuse that photo.
